## Tech Debt

### Phase 2: Duplicate rehydrateSessions() call
- **Files:** `src/index.ts:91`, `src/events/ready.ts:18`
- **Issue:** Both the `ready` event handler (fired during `client.login()`) and the explicit call after `login()` in `index.ts` call `rehydrateSessions()`. Idempotent but causes a redundant DB query on every startup.
- **Recommendation:** Remove one of the two calls.

### Phase 4: Extraction DLQ has no consumer
- **Files:** `src/lib/queue.ts`
- **Issue:** Failed extraction jobs land in the dead-letter queue (`extraction-dlq`) after max retries, but no worker or monitoring exists to process or alert on them.
- **Recommendation:** Add a DLQ consumer or implement alerting.

### Phase 5: Missing VERIFICATION.md
- **Issue:** Phase 5 has comprehensive UAT (7/7 passed) and 2 SUMMARY files, but no formal VERIFICATION.md document was created during execution.
